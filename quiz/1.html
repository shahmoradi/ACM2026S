Quiz 1

by Amir Shahmoradi — 08 Feb 2026


This quiz is open-lecture, however, you are not allowed to consult other resources or people.
You have 60 minutes to respond to the questions and submit your answers.


  1. (Score: 10) Show that the following identity follows directly from the Pythagorean theorem:

    \(\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta = 1\)

  2. (Score: 20) Prove the following identity:

    \(\sin(\alpha \pm \beta) = \sin(\alpha) \cos(\beta) \pm \cos(\alpha) \sin(\beta)\)

  3. (Score: 20) Prove the following identities hold:
    a. (Score: 10 / 20) The double cross-product identity (also known as the bac-cab rule):

    \({\bf a} \times ({\bf b} \times {\bf c}) = {\bf b} ({\bf a} \cdot {\bf c}) - {\bf c} ({\bf a} \cdot {\bf b})\)

    b. (Score: 10 / 20) The alternate double cross-product identity:

    \(({\bf a} \times {\bf b}) \times {\bf c} = {\bf b} ({\bf a} \cdot {\bf c}) - {\bf a} ({\bf b} \cdot {\bf c})\)

  4. (Score: 10) How many scalar multiplications are generally involved in the multiplication of two square matrices of rank $n$?

  5. (Score: 20) Write down the set of equations that convert a Cylindrical coordinate triplet to a Spherical triplet.

  6. (Score: 20) Show that $(\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b})^2 = a^2b^2 - (\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b})^2$.
